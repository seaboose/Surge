Decisions and tradeoffs

  • Explicit movement rules are easy to inspect, but cannot identify every automated behavior.
  • Missing evidence stays distinct from a clean observation.
  • Hashes detect changed evidence; they do not prove an untrusted client told the truth.
  • Unreal, Python and Electron suit different parts of the project, at the cost of a larger download.
