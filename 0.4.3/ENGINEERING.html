Tradeoffs

  • A controlled game gives reproducible experiments; it does not establish commercial anti-cheat effectiveness.
  • Explicit constraints are explainable; they do not classify every scripted behavior.
  • Hashes detect inconsistent retained evidence; they do not prove an untrusted producer reported reality truthfully.
  • Electron, Python and Unreal simplify distinct parts of the workflow but increase package size and integration work.
